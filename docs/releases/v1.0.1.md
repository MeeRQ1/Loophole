Loophole’s first release turns a handful of rules into a daily word ritual. There is no hidden answer: every accepted English word satisfying all the rules wins.

- Play three increasingly challenging Daily Run rounds for up to 500 points, with six guesses and one optional Nudge per round.
- Explore 396 scheduled days of verified content, endless practice, independent archive replays, and custom friend challenges.
- Keep your local streak, statistics, and 12 achievements; export a backup or share spoiler-free results as text or an image.
- Play offline after caching the game, install it through your browser, and choose light/dark themes, stronger contrast, reduced motion, or optional sound.

The game includes 241,528 accepted English entries, a 10,000-word common vocabulary, and 1,188 independently validated scheduled puzzles. All 22 rule types use one deterministic engine. Local progress is device-specific, and there are no accounts, ads, rankings, or tracking.

This release workflow verifies TypeScript, lint, unit tests, the complete content bank, a production build, browser gameplay, offline operation, and automated accessibility before publication. Dictionary licensing and regional word policies are documented in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.

The schedule covers the 30-day archive plus a full year beyond the October 31 launch. All previously deployed puzzle definitions remain unchanged.
